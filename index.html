Features

Built different

Everything a DEX aggregator should be, nothing it shouldn't

Smart Order Routing

Aggregates liquidity across 40+ DEXs and splits your order across multiple venues to guarantee the best possible execution price.

Non-Custodial

Your keys never leave your wallet. KeroSwap is a routing layer — we never hold, touch, or access your funds at any point.

Zero MEV Protection

Private transaction submission protects your swaps from sandwich attacks and frontrunning bots. Your trade, your profit.

Cross-Chain Native

Bridge and swap in a single transaction. Move assets between 12 chains without leaving the interface or paying double fees.

Best Rates Guaranteed

We compare every quote in real-time. If a better route exists anywhere on-chain, we find it and route through it.

Gas Optimized

Batched settlements, gasless approvals, and meta-transactions reduce overall swap cost by up to 40% compared to direct DEX calls.
